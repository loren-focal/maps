Scheduling

When enabled, scheduling determines when your heaters can operate. During these hours, heaters can be turned on by your staff, by your guests via QR code, and automatically when a guest is seated. Outside these hours, heaters stay off and can't be turned on. If scheduling is disabled, they will never turn on or off automatically. Learn more
